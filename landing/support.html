Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

With email and password, Google, or Sign in with Apple.

How do I delete my account?

Menu (⋮) on the home screen → "Delete account". See "Account deletion" below for details.

I'm not receiving the email confirmation.

Check your spam folder. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, email review.nepogano@gmail.com with the address you signed up with.

How do I add a friend via a link?

Open the link you received (nepogano.app/join/...) on a device with Nepogano installed. The app will open itself and offer to add the friend.

Account deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app:

  1. Open the Nepogano app.
  2. On the home screen, tap the menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose "Delete account".
  4. Confirm the deletion twice.

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately and irreversibly.

If you can't access the app — send a deletion request to review.nepogano@gmail.com with the email address of your account. Deletion will be handled manually within a few days.

What gets deleted: your email and authentication account; all mood entries (personal and any extra diaries, along with notes and photos); profile avatar; comments under days; time capsule letters you wrote or received; friendships and circle membership. No additional retention period.
