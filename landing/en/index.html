Not a feed of strangers.
People who actually matter.

Social media eats up time on other people's curated lives: thousands of people you don't even know. Nepogano deliberately has no public feed, no algorithm, no follower counts. Just the people you added yourself.

Honest check-in

One tap, a couple of words, sometimes a photo, and the day is recorded the way it actually was, no polish.

Extra diaries

Keep another diary, for a child or a pet for example, and open it up for someone in the family to see.

Friends and the guessing game

Add people close to you, try to guess their mood, and leave a comment under the day once you've guessed.

Day card

A minimal image of your day, to share with whoever you want, outside the feed and without likes.

A look at the month

Entries add up to a calm retrospective: no scores, just a mirror of your month.

Time capsules

Write a letter to yourself or a friend and seal it for a month, half a year, or a year. Nobody can read it, not even you.
